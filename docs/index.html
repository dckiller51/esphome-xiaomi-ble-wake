Xiaomi BLE wake

Wakes a Xiaomi TV or box from deep standby with an ESP32, the way its Bluetooth remote does.

1. Install: plug the ESP32 (ESP32, ESP32-C3 or ESP32-S3) into this computer with a data USB cable, then:

Your browser can't flash: use Chrome or Edge on a computer. Open this page over HTTPS.

Right after, the installer asks for your Wi-Fi. If it doesn't, connect to the Xiaomi BLE wake hotspot from a phone and pick your network.

2. Set the two addresses, on the ESP32's own page (http://xiaomi-ble-wake-xxxxxx.local, or its IP) or in Home Assistant once it's added:

  1. TV address: the TV's Bluetooth address (Settings › Device preferences › About › Status).
  2. Remote address: the TV's remote (adb shell getprop persist.bluetooth.wakeuprcaddr). The ESP32 restarts to take it.

How to find them: the guide.

3. Wake the TV: turn it off with its remote, wait 5 minutes, press Wake the TV on the ESP32's page or in Home Assistant.

Source, guide and sniffer: github.com/dckiller51/esphome-xiaomi-ble-wake
